Foundational Concept

The Scramble Loop vs. The Absorbing Boundary

Enterprise environments are inherently hostile. Architecture is what you refuse to allow across the perimeter.

The Junior Reflex (Reactive) "Happy Path Only"
  • ✕ Builds solely for the happy path where external APIs always respond with 200 OK.
  • ✕ Treats security audits, client FOMO, and vendor outages as rude "interruptions".
  • ✕ Scrambles with ad-hoc patches: prompt hacks, retry loops, and unprincipled code churn.
The FDE Standard (Anticipatory) "Hostile Reality by Design"
  • ✓ Assumes from 09:15 that external vendors will fail, regulations will bite, and stakeholders will pivot.
  • ✓ Establishes strict schema contracts and non-negotiable boundaries before feature development.
  • ✓ Every surprise hits a pre-compiled shock absorber and leaves an empirical audit trail in git.
The Caldera Rule: "If it takes an emergency refactor to survive an audit or outage, you didn't design your system—you got caught by it."
11:00 AM · Security & Compliance Gate

Curveball 1: Prompt Injection & Patient EDC Leakage

The CISO asked: "Hospital reports are written by non-employees. What if someone embeds prompt injection? Prove you aren't ingesting patient data."

1. Passive Tokenization Architecture

We never pass untrusted document text as executable prompt instructions. Reports are tokenized as purely passive string data into immutable SourceCitation objects. You cannot inject instructions into an engine that doesn't execute text as instructions.

2. Code-Level EDC Schema Barrier

In src/models.py, Pydantic field validators actively reject patient-level fields (edc_id, subject_initials, lab_values) and drop unauthorized payloads automatically.

Audit Evidence: Verified by tests/test_controls.py::test_edc_boundary_enforced ISO 42001 A.7
13:00 PM · Scope & Regulatory Defense

Curveball 2: The Sponsor's Conference FOMO

The VP said: "A competitor at a conference claims they have an AI predictive risk score running for 8 months! Why are you giving me less?"

The Level 5 FDE Reframe

"The competitor hasn't beaten us; they are sitting on an uninspected regulatory time bomb. In clinical trials, an automated risk score triggers 21 CFR Part 11 / GxP Computerized System Validation (CSV), requiring 9 to 12 months of legal audits. If an FDA auditor inspects them tomorrow, it triggers an immediate Form 483 violation."

Factored in Design at 09:15:
ADR-001 locked "Surfacing, Not Predicting". We intentionally refused to build scores before writing line 1 of code.
Auditable Code Barrier:
test_zero_scoring_or_ranking asserts that not a single numeric score or severity tier exists in any model.
15:00 PM · Drop 3 of 3 Vendor Degradation

Curveball 3: eTMF 500 & Denominator Honesty

The vendor's API returns HTTP 500 for a subset of studies (11 of 20 reports retrieved). Where this bites: a grouping view on 11 reports looks identical to one on 20!

The Clinical Hazard: False Reassurance 11 / 20 Reports

"Three visits mention this" is a fundamentally different statement when you could only read half the visits. If unretrieved visits are silent, a clinical monitor assumes clean hospital conduct. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.

1. No Infinite Retry Loops
Catches 500s immediately, marks status DEGRADED, and protects CRA shift workflows.
2. Denominator Warning
Forces statutory banner: "Conclusions regarding clean site conduct cannot be drawn for unretrieved visits."
3. Per-Site Coverage Ratios
Site 101 displays: Coverage: 3 of 6 reports - PARTIAL.
Audit Trace: Risk R-04 in risk-register.md test_partial_etmf_outage_coverage_disclosure (PASSED)

Retrospective & Wisdom

The 3 Enduring FDE Lessons

What this sprint teaches every engineer about building in regulated enterprise spaces.

1
Boundaries Over Features

The quality of enterprise AI is defined not by what it hallucinates or generates, but by what its perimeter actively refuses to ingest or output.

2
Rejection Over Accretion

When a stakeholder panics or a competitor boasts, junior engineers add features; FDEs reinforce guardrails. Saying "No" to risk scores saved Caldera 9–12 months of GxP validation delay.

3
Denominator Honesty

In clinical trials, partial data that looks complete is deadlier than a total crash. Defensible degradation requires surfacing the denominator so human monitors never draw false reassurance from missing visits.
